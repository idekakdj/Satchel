Everything Margin does

Typed and handwritten notes, filed in folders.

Folders in a sidebar

Nest folders as deep as you like in a sidebar that folds away when you need the whole page.

Pen-first handwriting

Apple Pencil and other styluses draw with pressure and tilt. Your palm and fingers scroll, they never scribble.

Type, then annotate

Every note has a text layer and an ink layer on the same page, so you can type notes and mark them up.

Paper styles

Blank, lined, grid or dotted pages. Pen, pencil, highlighter and a stroke eraser.

Saved as you go

Notes save while you write and open on any device you sign in on.

Find anything

Search titles and typed text across every folder.
