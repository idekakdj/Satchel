Everything Almanac does

Classes, deadlines and study sessions on one calendar.

Month, week and agenda

Switch views in a tap. Drag to move or resize events, and expand any day to see its full schedule.

Repeating classes, done right

Weekly timetables follow your semester dates and breaks. Change one class, or the whole series, and review the effect first.

Tasks with progress

Assignments, exams and study sessions become tasks. Tick them off and watch your weekly progress grow.

Reminders

Set reminders per item. Almanac shows them as browser notifications and keeps missed ones in an inbox.

Import and export

Bring in any .ics calendar, export to other apps, and keep full backups. Old C.C. Lime backups restore too.

Make it yours

Profile photo, preset or custom colour themes, and course colours that follow you everywhere.
